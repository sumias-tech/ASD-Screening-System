Video Upload Guidelines

Follow these tips to capture the best video for analysis.

  • Use a well-lit room with light facing the child.
  • Keep the background simple and clutter-free.
  • Avoid TV, music, or other distractions.
  • Place the camera at the child’s eye level.
  • Keep the camera steady (use a table or stand).
  • Maintain a distance of about 3–6 feet.
  • Avoid loose or baggy clothing.
  • Use clothing that contrasts with the background.
  • Record for at least 1–3 minutes.
  • Make sure the child stays within the frame.
  • Allow natural play or interaction (do not force actions).
Tip: Keep the session calm and natural so the analysis can focus on authentic behavior.
