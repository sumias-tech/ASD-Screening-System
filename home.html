About Our Platform

Our platform is designed to assist in the early identification of autism traits in children. The system includes Sessions and questionnaires that helps consultants can assess the child's Condition and Determine autism levels more accurately. Consultants can review the collected data, conduct evalutions, and record consultantion sessions, enabling continuous monitoring and informed decision-making.


All information is securely stored and systematically organized for easy access.Here the platform supports video-based observation to enhance behavioral analysis and improve the accuracy of assessments. We aim to provide an efficient platform for early autism detection and better support for the childrens .
